Welcome to NEXUS

Talk to nearby phones without internet, SIM or mobile network. Every phone you connect can also relay messages further, so the network grows with each device.

Allow permissions

Android needs your permission before this app can find and talk to nearby phones. Nothing leaves your phone to any server.

Network range

INFO

Each link is chosen automatically between Bluetooth LE, Bluetooth Classic and Wi-Fi (Direct / hotspot / LAN) depending on what both phones support. Typical direct range is roughly 10 m on Bluetooth and up to about 60 m on Wi-Fi in open space; walls reduce it. Relay nodes extend the reach hop by hop. Real range depends on your devices and surroundings.

Recent calls

Video is small and slow on purpose (a few frames per second) so it fits a Bluetooth or Wi-Fi link; it works best on a direct link, and the camera only runs while the app is open. Calls need a live route to the other phone. Over a direct link audio is reliable. Over relay hops it is experimental: expect delay and drops, and every relay phone must have relay mode on. Voice is 8 kHz telephone quality to keep the data rate low.

Privacy and storage

Bluetooth and Wi-Fi

Radios are managed by Android. Keep Bluetooth and Wi-Fi turned on while using the mesh.

Battery

INFO

Staying discoverable keeps radios awake and uses battery. Turn the mesh node off when you do not need it. Some phone makers aggressively stop background apps; if devices drop when the screen is off, allow unrestricted battery use for NEXUS Offline in Android settings.
