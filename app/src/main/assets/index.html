Network range

INFO

Each link is chosen automatically between Bluetooth LE, Bluetooth Classic and Wi-Fi (Direct / hotspot / LAN) depending on what both phones support. Typical direct range is roughly 10 m on Bluetooth and up to about 60 m on Wi-Fi in open space; walls reduce it. Relay nodes extend the reach hop by hop. Real range depends on your devices and surroundings.

Voice calls

Device-to-device audio

Not available yet

Offline voice calling is planned for Phase 5 and is not implemented in this build. There are no call controls here on purpose, so nothing pretends to work.

Reliable calling needs a low-latency audio stream over a direct Wi-Fi or Bluetooth link. Routing live audio through several relay hops adds delay and drops, so long-distance calls over the mesh will stay experimental when they are added.

Privacy and storage

Bluetooth and Wi-Fi

Radios are managed by Android. Keep Bluetooth and Wi-Fi turned on while using the mesh.

Battery

INFO

Staying discoverable keeps radios awake and uses battery. Turn the mesh node off when you do not need it. Some phone makers aggressively stop background apps; if devices drop when the screen is off, allow unrestricted battery use for NEXUS Offline in Android settings.
